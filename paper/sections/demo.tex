\section{Demonstration}
\label{sec:demo}
Visitors open LabelLens at \url{https://seungbum.github.io/labellens/} (no login). A typical walk-through takes three minutes. (1)~Paste a post link or a handle, or click an example; the census bar fills as \labEndpoint services are asked. (2)~Compare the four lens cards: the same item may be shown as is to a logged-out visitor, with an info badge in the popular stack and with a warning in the protective stack, each with the labels that caused it. (3)~Open \emph{your stack} and toggle labelers to see which one decides the outcome. (4)~Scan the per-labeler list, where struck-through labels were retracted, faded ones expired, and dashed ones have no effect. (5)~Switch to the Network page to see how few services are reachable and how unevenly labels are spread. At the conference, attendees can inspect their own accounts on our laptop or their phone; a two-minute video is linked from the site's About page and the repository, \url{https://github.com/seungbum/labellens}.
