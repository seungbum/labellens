On Bluesky, moderation is \emph{stackable}: anyone can run a labeling service, and each user chooses which of these \emph{labelers} to trust. Two people can therefore see the same post very differently, and nobody, including its author, sees the whole picture. We present LabelLens, a serverless web tool that asks every public labeler about a post or an account at once and renders the item through several subscription sets with the official client's moderation code, side by side. A companion page reports a network-wide measurement of all \labDeclared declared labelers. Only \labAnswering of their \labEndpoint published endpoints answer queries, and \labBrowserPctOfAnswering\% of those allow a browser to read the answer. Of the labelled posts in a uniform sample, \lensPostProtDiffPct\% look different to a user subscribed to the twenty most-liked protective labelers than to a logged-out visitor, \lensTopDriverPct\% of them because of a single service, while the twenty most-liked labelers overall change only \lensPostPopDiffPct\%. LabelLens runs from a link, with no login and no server.
